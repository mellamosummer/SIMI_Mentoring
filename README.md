## About the curriculum
Volunteers from across the Plant Sciences have worked together to develop an online cohort-based course designed to cultivate culturally sustaining mentoring practices in the Plant Sciences, the Social Identity Matters in Mentoring Program (SIMI Mentoring). The seven-module SIMI mentoring curriculum includes self-reflection activities, realistic case studies, and facilitated group discussions that support mentors at all stages in critically examining their mentoring practices. Here we present materials for use by course facilitators and course participants to guide them through the seven-week course. In addition to the full SIMI curriculum, we provide materials necessary to train facilitators. We offer this curriculum not only as a practical resource but as a model for disciplinary societies seeking to lead community-engaged equity efforts.

### For _participants_

This folder contains a syllabus for the course and folders with materials for each week of the training. The weekly folders contain a "one-pager", session agenda, and any assigned readings/materials. A combined PDF of "one-pagers" is also available for those interested in familiarizing themselves with the course materials in more detail.

```text
├── Syllabus/
│   └── SI-MI Course Syllabus
└── Weekly_Materials/
    ├── Session 1: Orientation/
    ├── Session 2: Identity and its Intersections/
    ├── Session 3: Engaging in Empathetic Conversations/
    ├── Session 4: Racialized Experiences in STEM/
    ├── Session 5: Color-Evasive Practices/
    ├── Session 6: From Deficit to Asset-Based Mentoring/
    └── Session 7: Embodying Culturally Responsive Mentoring/
```

### For _facilitators_

This folder contains materials for facilitating the program, including **surveys**: .pdf versions of course pre- and post-surveys and post-session feedback surveys for sessions 1-6, **attendance**: template for facilitators to sign up for particular sessions, and a template for tracking participant attendance, **email-templates**: e-mail templates for communicating with participants, **overview**: a facilitator guide that provides an overview of the course, expectations for facilitators, and instructions for facilitating the course, **syllabus**: a syllabus to be updated with dates, times, and zoom links for the course, and **weekly_facilitation_guides**: includes detailed session-by-session guides for facilitators as well as notes and tips from past facilitators.

```text
For_Program_Facilitators/
├── Attendance/
│   ├── Facilitator Sign-Up Sheet
│   └── Participants & Attendance 
├── Email_templates/
│   └── Email templates
├── Overview/
│   └── Facilitator Guide
├── Syllabus/
│   └── SI-MI Course Syllabus
└── Weekly_Facilitation_Guides/
    ├── Facilitator Tips_ Notes for All Sessions.pdf
    ├── Session 1: Orientation/
    ├── Session 2: Identity and its Intersections/
    ├── Session 3: Engaging in Empathetic Conversations/
    ├── Session 4: Racialized Experiences in STEM/
    ├── Session 5: Color-Evasive Practices/
    ├── Session 6: From Deficit to Asset-Based Mentoring/
    ├── Session 7: Embodying Culturally Responsive Mentoring/

```
